🧠 your second brain · knows your whole family · gets smarter every day
coming from spoony?
spoony is shutting down. if you're looking for a new home for your neurodivergent brain, this is it. spoonstack is different from spoony (it's a second brain, not a social community) but it was built for exactly the same people. welcome.
why spoonstack? spoon theory, coined by christine miserandino, describes energy as a finite daily resource. neurodivergent people start each day with fewer spoons and spend more of them on things that are free for neurotypical people. spoonstack helps you spend yours on what actually matters.
How it works

not another productivity app.

most productivity apps were built for neurotypical brains on good days. spoonstack was built for your actual brain, on your actual days. it handles the parts that cost you the most, learns your patterns, and adjusts when you're running low.
